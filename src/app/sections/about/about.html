About Me

From operations to cloud infrastructure.

My background combines operational leadership, software development and infrastructure engineering.

Before moving into IT, I worked for several years as a team lead in courier operations, coordinating daily processes and taking responsibility for a team.

I later transitioned into software development, where I gained experience with frontend development and collaborative team projects. During that time, I became increasingly interested in the systems behind applications: Linux, deployments, infrastructure and automation.

Today, I focus on DevOps and cloud infrastructure, building practical projects with AWS, Terraform, Ansible, Docker and Kubernetes. My goal is to create infrastructure that is reproducible, automated and reliable.
